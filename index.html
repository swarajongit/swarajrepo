Project 1 — BOM Management & Supply Chain Planning (7.2M RHB)
Chowgule Fiberglass Ships · L&T Defence Contract · 4 Boats · Delivery: 31 May 2026

Built the company's first structured BOM from scratch — decomposed the 7.2M boat into 382 distinct SKUs across 5 production stages (Hull → Sub-Assembly → Systems → Engine → Final/LSA). Mapped every item's lead time (2–120 days), PO release date, and stage-wise cost. Flagged 6 critical long-lead items (engine: 120 days, Hypalon collar fabric: 35 days). Built a Gantt-linked procurement calendar that ensured zero material-caused production stoppages across the 59-day production window. Stage 4 (Engine & Propulsion) identified as highest cost driver at ₹24.5L/boat (62% of total) — triggering early PO action 120 days before installation date. Production ran: 23 Mar → 23 May 2026.
